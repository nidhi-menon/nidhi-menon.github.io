The ML Community Must Operationalize and Evaluate Matrescence as a Construct in Its Own Right

Accepted

Nidhi Menon, Independent Researcher · ML4H 2026, Findings track (poster) · Position paper

Argues that ML label schemas reduce maternal mental health to depression versus healthy, with no category for matrescence, and that the ML community should operationalize it through construct-aware, lived-experience-informed datasets instead of waiting for clinical infrastructure.

Read the paper (PDF)

This is a position paper: no new empirical data or model results are reported. I am looking for matrescence and developmental-psychology researchers, maternal-health clinicians, machine learning researchers, and lived-experience annotators to help define, operationalize, and evaluate the construct.
