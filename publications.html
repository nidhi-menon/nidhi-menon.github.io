The ML Community Must Operationalize and Evaluate Matrescence as a Construct in Its Own Right

Accepted

ML4H 2026, Findings track (poster) · Position paper

Argues that ML label schemas reduce maternal mental health to depression versus healthy, with no category for matrescence, and that the ML community should operationalize it through construct-aware, lived-experience-informed datasets instead of waiting for clinical infrastructure.

HealthSteward: A Local-First Agentic Architecture for Patient-Side Care Coordination

Under review

IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics

Proposes four design principles for patient-side agentic care coordination, instantiates them in the open-source HealthSteward system, and evaluates PHI redaction, after-visit-summary parsing, disengagement detection, and visit-preparation groundedness.
